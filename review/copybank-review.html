What it changed, and what is yours to call

  
    
    
  • 
      Fixed
      
    Every campaign in the record ends at partial, not completed
    
      
    Not one of the 41 reached a clean sweep — a broadcast to the whole base always
      hits some dead token. The executor treated anything short of completed as a
      failure, so every push would have paged the failure topic, failed its run, and left the
      slot marked failed — where a rerun of the same cron would have sent it
      a second time. Delivery is now judged on the failure rate instead.
    
    
    • 
    
  • 
      Fixed
      
    A 48.5% failure is a token purge, not an outage
    
      
    Normal misses run 0.27–4.79% of the audience. The one exception, 19:22 on
      21 June, lost 1,887 of 3,891 — and the next send three hours later targeted 2,025,
      because those dead tokens were gone. Above 10% now raises an alert without failing the
      run, so a purge is visible but never re-sends the push.
    
    
    • 
    
  • 
      Your call
      
    Five a day is roughly six times the volume the base has ever seen
    
      
    The record averages 0.8 pushes a day and went silent for half of those 51 days. The
      new schedule sends five, every day, to a base that has tripled to 6,031 in seven weeks.
      The copy is ready for it; the audience has never been asked to take it.
    
    
    • 
    
  • 
      Your call
      
    Two in three historical pushes carried an image. Ours carry none.
    
      
    28 of 41 shipped with artwork. The send payload supports an image and the planner
      never sets one, so every push in the new schedule is text-only — a visibly plainer
      notification than the house style, five times a day.
    
    
    • 
    
  • 
      Your call
      
    The new copy still names the clock more often than the record did
    
      
    Only 5 of 41 pushes named a duration. The bank is down from 113 variants to 60, so
      roughly one send in three still states the cost up front against one in eight before.
      That gap is deliberate — a named length is the cheapest reason to tap — but it is
      the last real divergence left in the copy.
    
    • 
    
  • 
      Matched
      
    Emoji, verbs and the session name now sit where the record put them
    
      
    Emoji: 59% of the record, 68% of the bank. The session named in the title: 22% in both.
      Start leads 86 of 200 variants against play's 59, the same order as the
      record's Start 23 to Play 9 — and Start is used for guided sessions
      while soundscapes keep play and listen, exactly as the record did.

Two sentence shapes, three flavours
shipped copy

    
Strip the session names out and every one of the 41 pushes is one of two shapes. The
    flavours below sit inside the first one and recur often enough to be house style — the
    rewrite keeps one, drops another, and cannot reproduce the second shape at all.

    
    
    
  • Shape 1 — benefit, then the tap · 30 of 41Let go of stress. — Tap To Begin - Yog Nidra Session
    The house default, and the shape the new bank inherits: title earns the read, body spends it on one instruction. Only two sends fit neither shape, both of them evening wind-down copy with no instruction at all.
    • 
    
  • Shape 2 — the session name is the whole title · 9 of 41Deep Healing Nidra — Start Now for a Deep Sleep
    Works because members already know these names. The bank now opens 45 of its 200 titles with the session — the same 22% share — but never bare: {item} has to read for every item the slot can serve, so it always sits in a phrase.
    • 
    
  • Flavour — staccato commands · 6 of 41Relax. Breathe. Unwind. 🤍 — Begin your Yog Nidra session.
    Three one-word orders, then the session. The closest the old copy came to the new voice.
    • 
    
  • Flavour — stacked pipe CTA · 10 of 41Time to unwind. 🌙 — Evening Wind Down | Click Here
    A pipe standing in for a button. Reads as UI, not speech — the rewrite drops it.
    • 
    
  • Flavour — straight question · 3 of 41Racing Thought ? — Start Yog Nidra Session
    Rare, and each one is a symptom the session answers. Two carry a stray space before the question mark.
